Examwizards is a production-ready, role-based learning platform built on **Spring Boot** (Java 17) and **React 18**. Instructors publish courses and design timed assessments; students browse the catalog, enroll, pay where required, and sit exams that are auto-graded and emailed instantly. Admins oversee users, reviews, and platform analytics from a central dashboard.

## What Examwizards does

Examwizards ships three role-driven experiences in a single monorepo:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Students" icon="graduation-cap" href="/roles/student">
    Browse the course catalog, enroll for free or pay via Razorpay, take timed exams, and view scored results.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Instructors" icon="chalkboard-teacher" href="/roles/instructor">
    Create public or private courses, build question banks, publish exams, and track enrollment revenue.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Admins" icon="shield-halved" href="/roles/admin">
    Manage all users, moderate reviews, respond to contact requests, and monitor platform-wide analytics.

## Getting started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Clone and configure">
    Clone the repository and copy `.env.example` to `.env`, filling in your database, mail, Razorpay, and Gemini credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Spin up with Docker Compose">
    Run `docker compose up -d` to start MySQL, the Spring Boot API, the Gemini chatbot, and the React SPA — all on a shared bridge network.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your first user">
    Register at `http://localhost:3000`, verify your email, and log in. The bootstrap admin account is pre-seeded from your `.env` values.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore the API">
    Visit the [API Reference](/api/overview) to authenticate and call your first endpoint, or read the [role guides](/roles/student) to understand the platform flows.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The production target is **AWS ECS Fargate + RDS**. Docker Compose is for local development only. See [Deployment → AWS](/deployment/aws) for Terraform-based production setup.
</Note>
